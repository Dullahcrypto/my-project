Privacy policy

Neuromed Medical (“we”, “us”) is a neurology and neurodiagnostics clinic at the 2nd Floor, Annex Building, Arewa Specialist Hospital & Diagnostics Centre, Plot 645, Alex Ekwueme Way, Jabi, Abuja. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Booking details you give us: your name, phone number, the service you need and, if you choose to share them, your symptoms.
  • Messages and calls: what you tell us when you call or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may record standard server logs, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

Symptoms are health information, which the law treats as sensitive. Only share what you are comfortable sharing before your appointment.

How the booking form works

The booking form does not store your details on this website. When you press “Send request on WhatsApp”, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own phone or computer. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Why we use your information

  • To arrange, confirm and manage your appointment.
  • To prepare for your consultation or test.
  • To contact you about your care, results or follow-up.

We use your booking details because you have given your consent by ticking the consent box or contacting us, and because we need them to provide the care you asked for. We do not use your information for marketing, and we never sell it.

Who can see it

Only Neuromed staff who need your information to arrange or provide your care. We share it outside the clinic only where you agree, where it is needed for your care (for example, a referral you ask for), or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking messages only as long as needed to arrange your care. Once you become a patient, your medical records are kept for as long as Nigerian law and professional rules require.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete information we no longer need to keep;
  • stop using your information where you have withdrawn your consent.

You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you are unhappy with how we handle your information.

Third-party services on this website

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the map link opens Google Maps. These services may receive your IP address when you use them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, call 0813 102 6221 or 0810 007 0670, message us on WhatsApp, or speak to the front desk at the clinic.
