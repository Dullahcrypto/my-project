Who we care for

Every age. Every family in Abuja.

A child with seizures. A parent after a stroke. A grandparent with numb feet or a failing memory. Neurology touches the whole family, and so do we.

Neuromed is a neurology clinic in Jabi, Abuja. We find out what is happening in the brain and nerves, explain it plainly, and treat it, so patients and families are not passed from place to place.

Services

Diagnosis and treatment under one roof.

Your test, your results and your treatment plan come from the same neurologist, in the same building.

  • 01EEG & Video EEGRecords the brain’s electrical activity to diagnose seizures, epilepsy and other brain disorders. Video EEG captures an episode as it happens.
  • 02Electromyography (EMG)Shows how your muscles respond to nerve signals, to find the cause of weakness, cramps or muscle wasting.
  • 03Nerve conduction studiesMeasures how fast signals travel along your nerves, showing whether there is nerve damage and how far it goes. Useful for numbness and tingling.
  • 04Seizure & epilepsy controlAccurate diagnosis, the right medication and regular follow-up until seizures are under control.
  • 05Stroke managementAssessment, prevention of a second stroke, and recovery follow-up for patients and their families.
  • 06In-house pharmacyCollect your neurology medication before you leave, instead of searching pharmacies across Abuja.

Seizures & epilepsy

See what the brain is doing, then bring seizures under control.

A seizure is frightening for the person and for everyone around them. With video EEG we record the brain while an episode happens, identify the type of epilepsy, and choose medication that fits. Follow-up visits keep the plan working, and refills come from our pharmacy.
