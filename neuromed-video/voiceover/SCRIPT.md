# Voiceover script: Neuromed Clinic explainer

Tone: calm, warm, reassuring; a clinician you trust, not an advert. Unhurried.

| Time | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook | Seizures. Numbness. Weakness. Stroke. |
| 0:04 | Promise | Neuromed Clinic finds answers for the brain, nerves and muscles. |
| 0:08 | EEG | EEG and video EEG. |
| 0:11 | EMG | EMG for your muscles. |
| 0:13 | Nerve conduction | Nerve conduction studies. |
| 0:16 | Seizure & epilepsy | Seizure and epilepsy care. |
| 0:19 | Stroke | Stroke management. |
| 0:21 | Pharmacy | And medication from our own pharmacy. |
| 0:24 | Your visit | Book. Consult. Get tested on site. Then treat, and follow up. |
| 0:30 | Call to action | Book on WhatsApp: 0813 102 6221. Neuromed Clinic, Jabi, Abuja. |

About 80 words. The call to action needs ~6 s, so the end card is lengthened to fit
(see `npm run voice`). The exact text sent to ElevenLabs, with timings, is in
`lines.json` (letters like "E.E.G." are spelled with dots so they are read as letters).
